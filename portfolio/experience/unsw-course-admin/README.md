# Course Administrator at UNSW

***January 2023 - May 2026***

I administrated UNSW's COMP1010 course since the start of 2023 until its cancellation in 2026.

Through my administrative work, I significantly reimagined tutorial, laboratory and examination content to increase student engagement, as well as the quality and frequency of feedback that students received. In addition, I rewrote and created new course tooling to dramatically improve student-facing documentation and accessibility, as well as simplifying systems for staff to manage course content.
