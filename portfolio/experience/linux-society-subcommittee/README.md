# UNSW Linux Society Subcommittee

June 2026 - Present

I am a current member of the UNSW Linux Society subcommittee, where I am assisting with organisation and promotion of events, as well as development and maintenance of the society's open-source projects.

My contributions include:

## Live@LNSC

As well as [presenting](/blog/you-might-not-need-nixos), I helped to plan audio-visuals for Live@LNSC, the Linux society's community lightning talks. My responsibilities included:

* Directing two camera-people to help them capture the event from ideal angles.
* Running screen and audio capture.
* Briefing all presenters on hand-held and lavelier microphone technique to ensure their presentations were audible for audience members and the recording.
* Managing all AV equipment setup and tear-down with a very short timeline.

## Small promotional projects

I was the primary developer and designer of an online personality quiz we ran at [UNSW Hobbycon](https://web.archive.org/web/20260709133101/https://www.arc.unsw.edu.au/clubs/events/hobbycon), with the quiz being our society's primary approach for introducing ourselves and the world of Linux and open-source to convention attendees.

As I spend more time working with the society, I plan to contribute towards many more events and software projects.
